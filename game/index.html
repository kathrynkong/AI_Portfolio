❓ How to Play
  • Goal: win over two cats — Mochi and Professor Paws — by choosing how to interact with them.
  • Each cat has a mood: Curious, Sleepy, Grumpy, or Playful. Their portrait and reactions change with it.
  • Pet and Offer a Treat shift a cat's mood. Petting a Grumpy or Sleepy cat won't pay off — warm them up with a treat first.
  • A treat can also be stolen outright — the grumpier the cat, the more likely.
  • Ask a Question just gets you flavor/lore — no reward, no risk.
  • Professor Paws will Solve a Riddle with you: pick one of three answers. Wrong answers keep the same riddle so you can try again; a correct answer pays out and ends the riddle.
  • Paw Tokens are earned the first time an action wins a cat over. Once you've won a cat over with a given action, repeating it won't keep earning tokens — but the cat's still glad to see you.
  • Solve 3 riddles to unlock the 🏅 Riddle Sage badge.
  • Hit Play Again any time for a fresh round — dialogue and riddles are assembled differently each time.
